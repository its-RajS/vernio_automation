# AGENT RULES

Do not overengineer.

Do not introduce microservices.

Do not introduce NestJS.

Do not introduce queues.

Do not introduce event-driven architecture.

Do not introduce Redis.

Do not introduce Kafka.

Do not introduce WebSockets.

Do not introduce Docker unless explicitly required.

This application serves 4-10 users.

Prefer the simplest solution that works.

Always ask:

Can this be implemented directly in Next.js?

If yes:
Do not add another service.
